# Convert / spektrafilm attribution

NegativeScan Studio Convert (v0.93+) is **inspired by** the pipeline shape of
[spektrafilm](https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) by Andrea Volpato
(spectral film / print / scan simulation).

- spektrafilm **code** is GPLv3 — **not** used or linked here.
- spektrafilm **profiles and LUTs** are CC BY-SA 4.0 — **not** copied or ported
  into this MIT repository (share-alike is incompatible with silent MIT bundling).

Stock numbers in Convert come from public Kodak datasheets already digitised in
`colorsci/` and OFX gamma tables (`ofx/src/still_gammas.inc`), with sources cited
on each stock in the Convert UI and in `docs/convert-design.md`.
